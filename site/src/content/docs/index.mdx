Teams change agents constantly: a new system prompt, a different model, a new tool, a different router. Most judge those changes with LLM-as-judge scores or slow A/B tests. agentope estimates a change's effect on **real outcomes**, such as resolved tickets or purchases, from the logs you already keep.

## The question

> For one decision point, if a different policy had made the choice instead of the one that ran, and everything else had stayed the same, what would the average outcome have been?

The headline number is the **difference** between that estimate and what your agent actually achieved, with a 95% interval. Everything else in the report explains how far to trust it.

## Decision points

A decision point is one place where the agent picks from a few options:

| Decision point | Options | Who decides today |
|---|---|---|
| `tool_choice` | `search`, `lookup_order`, `escalate`, `__none__` | the LLM |
| `router` | `small-model`, `large-model` | code |
| `system_prompt` | `v1`, `v2` | a flag |

`__none__` is a real option: "the model answered without calling a tool" has to be a candidate, or tool-choice evaluation is biased. agentope evaluates **one decision point at a time** and holds everything else fixed, because importance weights multiplied over many steps have unusable variance.

## Evidence, not just a number

Off-policy evaluation needs to know how likely each logged choice was. Agents rarely record that, and token logprobs are not a reliable substitute, so agentope gets these probabilities in one of three ways and labels the result with its **evidence level**:

1. **Logged**: a little traffic was randomized, and the probability was recorded.
2. **Sampled**: the logging model is asked again on the stored input, N times.
3. **Estimated**: a model of the logging policy is fit from the logged context.

Each level states the assumptions it needs, and the report ends with a verdict: <Verdict kind="ok" />, <Verdict kind="weak" /> or <Verdict kind="unreliable" />. See [Evidence levels](/docs/evidence-levels).
